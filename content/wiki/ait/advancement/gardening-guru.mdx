---

To earn the "Gardening Guru" advancement, all you need to do is:
- Craft a [Coral Growth](../block/coral-growth)
- Successfully place the Coral Growth on top of a Soul Sand block in a Rift Chunk

The Coral Growth will not place if it is not on top of a Soul Sand block and in a Rift Chunk, which can be located with a [Rift Scanner](../item/rift-scanner).
