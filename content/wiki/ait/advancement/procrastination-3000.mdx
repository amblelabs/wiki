In order to earn the "Procrastination 3000" advancement:
- Craft a [Matrix Energizer](../tardis/matrix-energizer)
- Place the Matrix Energizer on a naturaly generated Sculk Shreiker, or any Sculk Shreiker the can summon a Wardon.

This Advancement is naturaly earned while obtaining a [Personality Matrix](../tardis/personality-matrix).
