---
title: Temporal Turbulance
description: Embrace the chaos of time and space by unintentionally crashing your TARDIS for the first time.
section: advancement
---

For this achievement you can get it by doing one of these few things:

- Slamming the hand brake while in flight
- Failing too many flight events
- Hitting controls too many time with the [mallet](../items/mallet)
