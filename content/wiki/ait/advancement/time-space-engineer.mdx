The advancement “**Time-Space Engineer**” can be achieved by:

1. Have an engine placed down inside your TARDIS.
2. Have Generalized Subsystem Cores **connected** to your [**Engine**](https://amblelabs.dev/ait-wiki/blocks/engine/).
3. Placing any component/subsystem inside the Generalized Subsystem Cores

As of 1.2.12, the advancemets are broken and it'll give you [Technical problems](technical-problems).
