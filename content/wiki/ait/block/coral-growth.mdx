A Coral Growth is crafted like so:

![](coral-growth/coral-growth-recipe.png)

The Coral Growth is the beginnings of a TARDIS. It can only be placed on Soul Sand inside of a Rift Chunk where it will begin to grow. Similar to crops in-game, the Coral Growth is grown in stages over time (this can be sped up by changing the game's random tick speed). Placing this block grants the player the [Gardening Guru](../advancement/gardening-guru) advancement.
