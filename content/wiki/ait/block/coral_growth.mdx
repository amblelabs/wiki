The Coral Growth is the beginings of the TARDIS. It is placed on Soul Sand inside of a Rift Chunk where it will begin to grow. Simular to crops in the game, the Coral Growth is grown in stages over time (this can be sped up by changing the games random tick speed). Placing this block grants the player the [Gardening Guru](../advancement/gardening-guru) advancement.
