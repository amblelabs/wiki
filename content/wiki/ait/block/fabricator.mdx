# **What is the Fabricator?**

The Fabricator is an essential block needed throughout the mod. It accepts [blueprints ](../item/blueprints)for crafting the components and subsystems needed to create your first TARDIS.

It can be crafted like so:

![](/images/fabricator_recipe.png)

Once crafted, the fabricator must be placed on top of a Smithing Table. 

**(Please note: It will not function without being on a smithing table)**

# **Can the fabricator do anything else?**

Yes! If you place a fabricator near a villager in the overworld, you can get a **Temporal Engineer Villager** which will allow you to trade emeralds for certain components in the mod.

**Dont put the villager in the TARDIS**, its technically always night in there  allow you to sleep at anytime to check your loyalty.
