The Plaque is crafted like so:

![](/images/plaque-recipe.png)

The Plaque is used for displaying manufacturing information of the TARDIS on your wall! It displays information such as the TARDIS’ name, the type, and the date it was created. It will only display the information relevant to the current TARDIS and will only work inside the TARDIS interior dimension.

You can change the TARDIS type by `right-clicking` the plaque with a name tag.
