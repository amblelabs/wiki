description: Turns combustible fuel into usable Artron energy.
section: block
---

The power converter is crafted like so:

![](/images/power-converter-recipe.png)

The power converter can turn [coral blocks](coral), [zeiton items](../item/zeiton), and vanilla minecraft fuel sources (such as: coal, lava bucket, wood, etc...) into artron energy for the TARDIS.

**Please Note: The power converter must be connected to the TARDIS engine via Artron Cables to work properly**
