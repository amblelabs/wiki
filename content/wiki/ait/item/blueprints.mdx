## What do i do with it?

With a blueprint you'll first need a fabricator, once you set up the fabricator, use the blueprint on it and give it the items you see cycling through by `right-clicking.` 

## Where do i find them?

You can find blueprints in most structures that has a chest, these include but are not limited too: Shipwrecks, Nether Fortresses, Villages, etc...
