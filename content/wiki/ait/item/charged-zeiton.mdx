## What is this?

To use it, simply hold the item in your hand and `right-click` 
the [Artron Collecter Block](../block/artron-collector) and it will drain the block. To fill up 
your TARDIS with it, `crouch right-click` the console base.

You can also see that it holds 5000 artron energy in the tooltip.

## Why cant i just use normal zeiton?

Normal zeiton hasn't been modified to actually be able to hold up the artron energy.
