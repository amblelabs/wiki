# **Subsystems/circuits**

These subsystems/circuits are needed to activate and or use certain functions of the TARDIS.

## How do i use them? 

They are used to access certain functions of the TARDIS, how? By inserting them (with `right click`) into the Generalized Subsystem Core.

- **WARNING circuits have durability.**
Click [**here**](../tardis/subsystem-durability/) to learn how to repair.

## How do i make them? 

Blueprints and the fabricator!

## What does each circuit do? 

- Demat circuit; needed to demateralize and remateralize.
- Backup circuit; BROKEN
- Chameleon circuit; needed to switch exteriors, cloak and use the adaptive exterior.
- Desperation circuit; allows the usage of siege mode/protocols.
- Gravitational circuit; unlocks anti-mavs.
- Life support; lets you breathe in the TARDIS and gives constant regeneration within the TARDIS.
- Stabilizers; unlocks P116 (auto-pilot)
- Shield circuit; unlocks both shield types

## Interior reconfiguration 

When you change interior, all components you had will be in the TARDIS console itself, if you right-click on the console will open a GUI and will have all the stuff you had inside
