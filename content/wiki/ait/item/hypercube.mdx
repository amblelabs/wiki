The Hypercube is used to transmit an SOS signal to every TARDIS on a server, when you `right click` it, 
it will pop like a totem and send a cube to other TARDIS' doors.
When a player picks it up, they will see it has 100 durability which will decrease overtime.

To accept the distress call, `right click` the telepathic circuits with the Hypercube you received and the 
TARDIS will go to the location of the person who sent it.

**Note**: To not recieve Hypercubes, you can disable them in the security settings of your TARDIS
by opening your monitor.
