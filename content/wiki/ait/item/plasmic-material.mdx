What's it for?

Plasmic Material has 2 uses

1. It is used in the recipes for the Coral Cage and the Food Machine.

2. It is to fill the Coral Cage after it has been place over a fully grown [Coral Growth](../block/coral-growth). You'll need 8 to fill in the cage completely.
