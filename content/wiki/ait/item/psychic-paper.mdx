  **What Does The Psychic Paper Do?**

It does 2 things;

1. it grants "Hero of the Village" status effect, making trades much cheaper. 
2. It changes your in-game name. This happens if you rename the Psychic Paper in an anvil, then holding it in your offhand or main hand.
