The **Rift Scanner** is a hand-held device that acts very similarly to a compass, but homes in on [**Rift Chunks**](../tardis/rift-chunks). To locate [**Rift Chunks**](../tardis/rift-chunks) using this device, simply `right-click` the rift scanner in your hand and follow the dial to you destination. (The ‘Geiger Counter’ is just a reskin of this, and for now is uncraftable) The device will point to the centre of the [**Rift Chunks**](../tardis/rift-chunks), when you are at the centre it will spin frantically.
