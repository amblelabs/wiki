The Sonic Screwdriver is a handheld device used for interacting with the TARDIS and the world around you. To craft it, a blueprint found in chests around the world is required.

## Recipe

![Sonic Screwdriver Recipe](/images/sonic-screwdriver-recipe.png)

To link a sonic screwdriver to a [TARDIS console](../block/console-1) right-click on the sonic port whilst holding the sonic screwdriver.  

To remove it, <Kbd>shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>right click</kbd> or <Kbd>left click</Kbd> the sonic screwdriver in the sonic port, and it will eject from the [console](../tardis/console).

To charge a sonic screwdriver, insert it into a [TARDIS console](../tardis/console) as one would for linking it to the TARDIS. The sonic screwdriver saps [artron energy](../tardis/artron-energy/) from the TARDIS it is inserted into for charging. When unpowered, a sonic screwdriver will be set to inactive mode and cannot switch modes until charged.



The sonic has 5 modes:

- Inactive
- Interaction
- Overload
- Scanning
- TARDIS

To change between modes, <Kbd>shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>right click</Kbd> with the sonic in hand.

## Inactive Mode

Inactive mode is default when a sonic screwdriver has no power.

## Interaction Mode

Interaction mode allows the sonic screwdriver to:

- Ignite candles and unlit torches
- Open iron doors/trap doors
- Change redstone repeaters' delay
- Change redstone comparators' mode

## Overload Mode

Overload mode allows the sonic screwdriver to:

- Ignite TNT
- Toggle redstone lamps
- Shatter glass blocks

## Scanning Mode

Scanning mode allows the Sonic Screwdriver to:

- Tell the player what controls they’re looking at on the console by hovering over the control entities
- Check if you are located inside a [rift chunk](../tardis/rift-chunks) and how much [artron energy](../tardis/artron-energy/) is stored in that chunk
- Tell the name and the durability of the subsystem held within a generalized subsystem core; if a particular subsystem can't lose durability it will only show the name of that subsystem
- Scan blocks and entities for health, required mining tool, and blast resistance
- Tell where your TARDIS is via <Kbd>right click</kbd> on a Zeiton Cluster or Bell

## TARDIS Mode

TARDIS mode provides the following features:

When inside the TARDIS with the sonic in your offhand:

- Looking up and using the sonic triggers demat
- Looking down and using the sonic activates the handbrake and refueler

When outside the TARDIS:

- Summon the TARDIS by <Kbd>right click</Kbd> a valid landing spot near you, within 256 blocks of the TARDIS exterior        
- Looking up with the sonic in the off-hand and using the sonic will trigger the demat
- Looking down with the sonic in the off-hand and using the sonic activates the handbrake and refuels the TARDIS

## Screwdriver Customization

- Place the Sonic Screwdriver into the sonic port
- Open the Monitor
- Cycle to the selection screen
- Select the "Sonic Settings" menu
- Use the arrow keys to cycle between options
- Use the blank button between them to confirm your changes

More sonic casings can be added with [datapacks](../datapacks/custom-sonics/).

## Sonic TARDIS Repair

After crashing your TARDIS, waiting on repairs can be time-consuming, but there is an option to speed up the repair process.

To do this, hold your sonic screwdriver in interaction mode and right click the TARDIS exterior. The sonic screwdriver will be inserted into the keyhole. If you right click the TARDIS exterior when it's repairing, the Sonic Screwdriver will be removed, and a pop-up will tell you how many seconds there are until the TARDIS repair has finished. This **will not** reset your interior. Once the repair has finished, a chime will sound to indicate the repairs have concluded, after which point you can get your screwdriver back via right click.
