## What is zeiton?

Zeiton is the main type of material this mod adds. There is the following variations

- Zeiton dust
- Zeiton block
- Zeiton budding
- All the types of zeiton growth
- [Charged zeiton crystal](charged-zeiton)

## How do I find it?

Much like amethyst geodes, zeiton geodes can be found in caves.

## What do I use zeiton for?

1. Zeiton is a part of the crafting recipe for many TARDIS components
2. Used for the [**Sonic Screwdriver**](sonic-screwdriver)
3. Used to get [charged zeiton](charged-zeiton)
4. Zeiton dust 
5. Superheated zeiton
6. Compact zeiton
7. Zeiton cobble

## Zeiton dust

A type of food, when eating it, you'll get zeiton high 100% of the time, there is also a chance you either get speed or wither from it due to its artronic charge, you'll also get other negative effects, such as: hunger, mining fatigue and nausea for a second

## How do i get the zeiton cluster?

You need silk touch for this one.

## Can I grow zeiton?

Yeah, zeiton budding can grow zeiton very slowly!
