## Compatible Mods 

List of compatible mods for Adventures in Time

- [Gravity Changer (qouteall fork)](https://modrinth.com/mod/gravity-api-fork) - allows for changing interior gravity direction.
- [Immersive Portals](https://modrinth.com/mod/immersiveportals) (_compatible in beta builds only, accessible via donation_) - allows for real BOTI (bigger on the inside) and SOTO (smaller on the outside) with seamless transitions.
- If you have [Adventures in Space](https://github.com/DrTheodor/adventures-in-space) and [Ad Astra](https://modrinth.com/mod/ad-astra), shields act as oxygen fields, while the spacesuit allows you to breathe in space. AIS has been deprecated since 1.5.0.
