## Compatible Mods 

List of compatible mods for Adventures in Time

- [Gravity Changer (qouteall fork)](https://modrinth.com/mod/gravity-api-fork)
- [Immersive Portals](https://modrinth.com/mod/immersiveportals) _Compatibility in Betas only_
- If you have [Adventures in Space](https://github.com/DrTheodor/adventures-in-space) and [Ad Astra](https://modrinth.com/mod/ad-astra), there is special compatibility with the TARDIS shields and AIT spacesuit when playing with Ad Astra
