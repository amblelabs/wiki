The moon is a moon only accessible through the TARDIS, as we don't have rockets. Its a planet that doesn't have a breathable atmosphere so you'll need a [space suit](../../item/space-suits) to navigate safely, or use an [oxygenator](../../block/oxygenator) block to get a small breathable atmosphere.

There are 2 structures that spawn here, the Moon Lander and the Cult structure.
