### Q: What do I choose: datapack or mod?

A: If you want to add interiors, demats/remats, exterior and console variants, etc., then feel free to do a datapack. Otherwise, making a mod is recommended (you can also do a datapack inside a mod).

### Q: How do I make a datapack/mod

A: Refer to the Datapacks & Addons section.

### Q: I changed my interior, and now the TARDIS won't power on!

A: You need to set up the engine again.

### Q: What mods are incompatible with AiT?

A: Below are the currently known incompatibilities. If you know any that are not listed here, please let us know!

- Immersive Portals: stops people from entering/exiting the TARDIS.
- No Nametag Render: will stop the control names from showing.
- Concurrent Chunk Management Engine (C2ME): seems to break rift chunks and TARDIS growth.
- Distant Horizons (serverside): breaks TARDIS growth, causes server crashes when entering a TARDIS. Client-side plays like normal.
- Valkyrien Skies (Forge/Fabric): will instantly crash your game when you place a TARDIS.
- Crackers Witherstorm Mod: Crashes on load.

### Q: Is RWF / ARS / BOTI / other being added? 

A: A lot of stuff is planned for the mod, but these are a few of the most common features that people might ask about: RWF: Not being worked on right now, but will eventually be worked on. It is not a priority at the moment. ARS: Yes. It won the poll and has seen some work done on it by the developers. BOTI: Yes. It also won the poll alongside ARS and has seen work done on it as well.

### Q: What is RWF/ARS/BOTI? 

A: RWF: Real world flight, picture that scene in series four where the TARDIS flies next to a taxi. ARS: Architectural Reconfiguration Systems, basically being able to add more rooms other than the desktop to your TARDIS. BOTI: Bigger On The Inside, kinda self explanatory, the bigger on the inside effect you see on the show.

### Q: How do I refuel? 

A: There’s two ways to refuel, 1, you can turn on the handbrake then press the refueler on the console, 2, you can attach an energy converter to the engine of your TARDIS and supply fuel via that.
