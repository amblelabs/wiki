So you want to get a TARDIS, but you don’t know how, well, you’re in the right place now.

Before anything, we suggest you at least get a lot of iron for a pickaxe and recipes that will come later.

## 1. Getting zeiton

[Zeiton](../item/zeiton) is found underground and is similar to amethyst geodes, as it also spawns in geodes (refer to the zeiton page to learn what it can do and how to grow more)

After getting some, you’ll need to craft a fabricator to be able to get the [components](../item/components) needed for a TARDIS.

## 2. Getting blueprints

[Blueprints](../item/blueprints) are what allows you to craft components, it can be found in most structures. You’ll need to gather each blueprint at least once as it is not an expendable item. 

## 3. Getting a Sonic

You’ll need to find a sonic screwdriver blueprint to make one, `right-click` the blueprint on the fabricator and you can see items flashing on it, those  are whats needed to craft the screwdriver. You can also see what you need by holding `shift` when hovering over a blueprint in the inventory.

## TARDIS Growth

You may refer to [this](../tardis/tardis-growth) page for help

## 4. Engine, Subsystems and Console

Now, assuming you followed the TARDIS Growth page and returned here with a empty TARDIS, you’ll need to get an engine to give your TARDIS power, a console to control it, and subsystems to add features to the thing.
