Cult Structures can be found in every dimension in vanilla minecraft and AIT dimensions except for the end.

They contain chests which can spawn with the following items:

## Vanilla Minecraft Items

- emeralds
- iron sword
- redstone repeater
- sculk
- cobweb
- gold nugget
- mourner pottery sherd
- blade pottery sherd
- redstone comparator
- redstone dust
