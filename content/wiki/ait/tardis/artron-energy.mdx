Artron Energy is the fuel the TARDIS runs on. It is measured using _Artron Units_, or _AU,_ and it can be obtained through rifts in the Overworld, or passively in any dimension by refueling. 

Where Is It Collected From? 

**Artron Energy** is collected by the TARDIS during **refueling**, and can be passively gained from anywhere (slowly) or gained in larger amounts quicker in a [**rift chunk**](rift-chunks). It can also be collected by [**Artron Collectors**](../block/artron-collector), which come in block and item form.
