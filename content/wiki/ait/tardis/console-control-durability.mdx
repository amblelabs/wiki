## How Does it Work? 

During normal flight, controls will break down over time.
You must either use a mallet on them to bypass any breakage, or use a [**Sonic Screwdriver**](../item/sonic-screwdriver/) in TARDIS mode to repair the control.

## How Will I Know What State It’s In? 

If there are no paricles above the controls, it’s fine. If it’s smoking, on fire, sparking, etc., it needs repair.

## Different States of Durability 

1. FULL - Max durability, normal controls
2. OCCASIONALLY_JAM - Occasionally jams
3. SPARKING - Sparks, won’t let you use it occasionally
4. CATCH_FIRE - Catches fire, won’t let you use it occasionally
5. JAMMED - Won’t let you use it

## How To Fix it

To fix it, use a [Sonic Screwdriver](../item/sonic-screwdriver) in TARDIS Mode on any broken control to fix it fully, but, if you need to use that control quick, you can use the Mallet (hammer) and force the control to execute its part of the flight event.
