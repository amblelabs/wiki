Desktop Settings button:

## Changing the Exterior 

1. Open the monitor by using the console or monitor block.
2. Use the **white arrows** to change the exterior **type**.
3. Use the **purple arrows** to change the exterior **variant**.
4. To save your changes, click **“Apply”**.

***

## Changing the Interior 

1. Open the monitor by using the control or monitor block.
2. Navigate to the interior change screen by clicking **“Desktop Settings” -> “Change Interior”**.
3. Use the arrows to change the selected interior.
4. To start **Interior Reconfiguration**, click the image of the desktop you want.
5. The process will use up 5000 AU
6. Exit the interior, and wait for the cloister bells to stop ringing. You’re also able to add your own custom desktops with [**Datapacks**](https://amblelabs.dev/ait-wiki/datapacks/custom-interiors/).

***

## Changing the Hum 

1. Open the monitor by using the control or monitor block.
2. Navigate to the desktop settings screen by pressing the button
3. Use the arrows to change the selected hum
4. Click **“Apply”** to select the active hum.

## Changing the Name 

To change the TARDIS name, follow these steps:

1. Rename a **name tag** to the desired nickname
2. Right-click the **renamed name tag** on the **Telepathic Circuits**.
3. Enjoy!

## Changing the sonic casing

1. Place your sonic in the sonic port
2. Open the monitor and click the desktop settings button
3. Click on sonic settings
4. Use the arrows to select the casing

## Changing the Vortex 

1. Open the monitor
2. Go to the desktop settings
3. Switch between the hum tab
4. When you see the vortex tab appear select the vortex you want.
5. Enjoy!

## What is the Vortex?

The Time Vortex is the dimension the TARDIS travels through in flight. It comes in all shapes, colors and sizes and may be customized in the monitor.

## **_Can you see the Time Vortex?_**

Yes, you can! Just open your doors while in flight or use the Environment Projector set to the `ait:time_vortex`skybox. I suggest turning on the [shields](shields), otherwise you'll be sucked out of your TARDIS.
