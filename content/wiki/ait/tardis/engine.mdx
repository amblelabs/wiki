The engine's coral will grow depending on the amount of components you have connected to it.

## How Do I Use It?

The Engine requires a free 3x3 space to be placed down. Place it anywhere inside the TARDIS and attach components to it using [Artron Cables](artron-cable) and [Generalized Subsystem Cores](../blocks/generalized-subsystem-core/).

 **The engine slowly loses durability when in use. Check out the** [Durability](subsystem-durability) **page for how to repair it.**

## How Do I Get It?

It can be crafted by using an engine blueprint on the **Fabricator** and then providing the required materials.
