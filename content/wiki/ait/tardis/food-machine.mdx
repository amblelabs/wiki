A food machine is crafted using the blueprint and fabricator, needing:

![](/images/food-machine-recipe.png)

When placed inside a TARDIS, it'll connect to the TARDIS's fuel supply automatically and spend artron energy to make food for you.

## Types of Food

There are 3 things the food machine can make: food cubes, overcharged food cubes and drinks.

Overcharged food cubes will give you 2 saturation and 2 hunger, and also the following effects:

- Regeneration 2 for 3 seconds
- Absorption 4 for 1 minute
- Resistance for 2 minutes 30 seconds
- Fire resistance for 2 minutes 30 seconds

Food cubes will give you 0.5 saturation and 1.5 hunger.

There are a few drinks in this mod, click here to learn how to add more drinks using datapacks. When you're done drinking it, it will leave behind an empty mug in your inventory.

- Iced coffee
- Water (but in a mug)
- Vodka
- Hot cocoa
- Coffee
- Latte
- Chocolate milk
- Tea
- Milk (but in a mug)
