## How to link items 

On the console, find the **Telepathic Circuits** and right click it with the linkable item. Linkable items include:

- [**TARDIS Keys**](../item/keys/)
- [**Sonic Screwdrivers**](../item/sonic-screwdriver/)
- [**Stattenheim Remotes**](../item/stattenheim-remote/)
- [**Handles**](../item/handles/)

## Why isn’t my Sonic linking? 

For the Sonic Screwdriver specifically, you will need to insert it into the **Sonic Port** for it to link properly.
If the sonic is already linked to another TARDIS, you must hold shift whilst inserting it in the sonic port to relink it, otherwise it will just charge in the port.

## Any useful commands? 

Certainly! The command to link different linkable items (like the sonic/keys/stattenheim remote) is: `/ait link <id or ~ or ^>` You'll need to hold the item you want to link when doing this command.
