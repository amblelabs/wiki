Your loyalty with your TARDIS will change depending on how you use it and treat it
There are 5 ranks, which are:

- Owner
- Pilot
- Companion
- Neutral
- Reject

Each rank requires a certain amount of loyalty points to obtain

Owner: 500
Pilot: 450-499
Companion: 245-449
Neutral: 125-244
Reject: 0-124

What increases my loyalty points?

- Doing flight events correctly
- Repairing a subsystem/circuit
- Fixing the TARDIS after a crash
- Passive increase while inside the interior

What decreases loyalty points?

- Missing flight events
- Crashing the TARDIS
- Letting water through the door
- Hitting the console with a hammer
- A key getting destroyed

> [!WARNING]
> Letting Water Seep into the TARDIS will cause rapid loyalty decrease

What does loyalty give me/do?

- Unlocks exterior variants
- Unlocks console variants
- Unlocks Sonic Screwdriver shells
- If you're a reject the alarms will turn on
Unlocks snapping for fingers
- You can choose which ranks aren’t affected by P19.
- If you're a companion the shields wont push you away anymore
- Makes it so P813 actually activates, however you still need a key
- At owner loyalty the shields turn on as well if P813 is triggered.

How do I see my loyalty?

Sleep in a bed inside the TARDIS, a sound will play indicating your loyalty rank and a message will appear as well.
