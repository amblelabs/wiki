## Real World Flight (RWF) 

Real world flight allows you to fly around the world in your TARDIS without dematerializing and rematerializing.

## How To Use RWF 
```plain
Firstly run /ait-config and check that RWF is enabled in your world.
```

then, you must use the command:

```plain
/ait flight [TARDIS ID]
```

then you will see the outside of your TARDIS like this:

To start flying press the ‘H’ key, this will turn on the antigravs, then to actually start moving around press the ‘G’ key then press space to go up, press the ‘B’ key to slow down, that’s about it.
