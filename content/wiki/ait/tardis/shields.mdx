## How do I use the shields? 

The shields have 2 modes:

1. Invisible

- Invisible shields block liquids from entering and stop you from getting sucked out into the vortex if your doors are open during flight.
- Activate them by `right clicking` the shields control once.

2. Visible

- The Visible ones block players under the companion loyalty level from entering your TARDIS, they also incinerate any sort of projectile and will push back any type of entity that isn't a player.
- Activate them by `shift + left clicking` or just `left clicking` the shield control when the invisible shields are active.

Once you activate the shields they start off by default as invisible. When both the Shields and Desperation circuits are installed, activating the shields will produce a breathable atmosphere around the TARDIS. This will allow breathing underwater when near the TARDIS.
**_NOTE: SHIELDS USE ARTRON ENERGY WHILE ACTIVE, MAKE SURE TO CHECK YOUR FUEL SUPPLY BEFORE ACTIVATING THIS PROTOCOL._** (Or just refuel and turn on the shields at the same time.)
