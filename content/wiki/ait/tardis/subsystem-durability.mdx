## WHY MUST YOU DO THIS TO US? 

Yes yes, I get it durability might seem scary at first but fear not as our durability isn’t a major pain in the bum.

- Each Subsystem/circuit (including the engine) has a max durability of 1250.
- To increase durability simply `right click` the subsystem/circuit with copper/gold/iron ingot, this increases the durability by 50.
- Not all subsystems/circuits have durability.
- Subsystems/circuits do not break when they get to 0 durability, they just stop working until you repair them.
- The only exception for this is the dematerialization circuit which can break due to engine phasing.

## What if I run out of durability? 

Depends, circuits/subsystems won’t break if you run out of durability but they’ll malfunction, as an example:

- Chameleon circuit will randomize instead of selecting the designated exterior.
- Dematerialization circuit can cause engines to [phase](../tardis/engine-phasing/) if it is  under 300 durability.

## How do I see durability? 

[**Sonic**](../item/sonic-screwdriver/) in scanning mode.

## How does durability go down? 

- For the engine, durability goes down passively over time.
- For the other circuits/subsystems that have durability, they’ll go down if the system they power is used (example: if you fly and dematerialize, the demat circuits durability goes down).
