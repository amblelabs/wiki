## Obtaining Coral

![](/images/rift-and-coral.png)

The first step to creating a TARDIS is the materials; you'll need nine coral fragments, which can be crafted into a [Coral Growth](../block/coral-growth). You can obtain coral shards by finding a [Rift](../tardis/rift-chunks). _How do I find a rift?_ You can find one by using and following the [Rift Scanner](../item/rift-scanner). Once you find one, you can right-click on it with your [Sonic Screwdriver](../item/keys). It's not an 100% chance you get a coral fragment though, but you can use the sonic on it to get coral fragments 100% of the time. Sometimes, coral blocks will spawn around the rift, and other times, paper will fall out of the rift. Once you have all nine, you can place them in a crafting table and get the first building block of your (soon-to-be) TARDIS.

## Planting the Coral Growth

![](/images/coral-growth.png)

Once you have a [Coral Growth](../block/coral-growth), you'll need to plant it. To do that, you'll need a block of soul sand and a rift chunk. Once you're in a rift chunk, place the soul sand, then put the Coral Growth on top and wait for it to grow. Once it's finished, it will open up and let you inside.

## The Personality Matrix

![](/images/shreiker-matrix.png)

<Callout title="Warning" type="warn">
  The shrieker must be naturally generated and not interacted with by any player.
</Callout>

The last thing you'll need is the Personality Matrix. To get one, you must first craft a [Matrix Energizer](../block/matrix-energizer). Once you have that, you'll need a Nether Star. Bring both to a sculk biome or ancient city, place the energizer on top of a shrieker, and insert the nether star. After a bit, the energizer will disappear, and a personality matrix will pop out! Bring it back to your TARDIS and drop it in the water inside. The TARDIS will begin a reconfiguration process, leaving it a clean slate for you to start your journey! You'll need a [Respirator](../item/respirators) to breathe inside the TARDIS.
