## Obtaining Coral



The first step to creating a TARDIS is the materials; you'll need nine coral fragments, which can be crafted into a [Coral Growth](../block/coral-growth). You can obtain coral shards by finding a [Rift](../tardis/rift-chunks). _How do I find a rift?_ You can find one by using and following the [Rift Scanner](../item/rift-scanner). Once you find one, you can right-click on it with your [Sonic Screwdriver](../item/keys). It's not an 100% chance you get a coral fragment, though. Sometimes, coral blocks will spawn around the rift, and other times, paper will fall out of the rift. Once you have all nine, you can place them in a crafting table and get the first building block of your (soon-to-be) TARDIS.

## Planting the Coral Growth



Once you have a [Coral Growth](../block/coral-growth), you'll need to plant it. To do that, you'll need a block of soul sand and a rift chunk. Once you're in one place, place the soul sand, then put the Coral Growth on top and wait for it to grow. Once it's finished, it will open up and let you inside.
