This page will teach you on how to repair your TARDIS after crashing.

## Tardis Crash

There are a few things that can cause a TARDIS to crash, such as: 

- not doing flight events,
- pulling the handbrake during flight, 
- running out of power during flight, etc…

After the crash, you’ll need a [respirator](../item/respirators) to breathe and do the following instructions while not dying.

## Repairing the TARDIS

To repair the TARDIS, you’ll need to turn the handbrake on and turn on the refueler to make it repair faster, then, go outside your TARDIS, make sure both doors are closed and put your [sonic](../item/sonic-screwdriver) in INTERACTION mode and right-click on the exterior.

When you `right-click` the exterior again after placing the sonic in the exterior, you can see seconds counting down, that’s how long until the TARDIS is repaired.

When it's done repairing, you’ll need to `shift` + `right-click` with an empty hand to take the sonic out of the keyhole and you can go back inside.

## Forced Entry

If you are locked out of your TARDIS and don’t have a key, you can use an iron axe or above to break the doors down, but this only works when the TARDIS is powered down.

<Callout title="Loyalty decrease" type="NOTE">
Using the forced entry option will negatively affect your TARDIS loyalty.
</Callout>
