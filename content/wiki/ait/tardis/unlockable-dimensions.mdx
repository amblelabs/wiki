---
title: Unlockable Dimensions
description: Every dimension except the overworld will be locked
section: tardis
---

Vanilla dimensions (Nether and The End) are locked. Why? To add balance to the mod since getting a TARDIS is easy thanks to blueprints spawning everywhere. Each dimension is unlocked in its own unique way. For the Nether, `right click` the telepathic circuits with a Blaze Rod. For the End, kill the Ender Dragon (this will unlock the End for everyone, but the Nether is unlocked per TARDIS)
