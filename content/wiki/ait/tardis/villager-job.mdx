# **Fabricator engineer**

The Fabricator engineer is a villager that sells various items and blueprints for your TARDIS. To get the Fabricator engineer, put a villager in front of a Fabricator.

The items offered by the villager depend on its profession level, they will decrease their price if you have psychic paper, just like with vanilla villagers.
Here is a list of possible items:

### Level 1 

- [R](../item/rift-scanner)[ift scanner](../item/rift-scanner)
- [Zeiton](../item/zeiton) shard
- [Hyperion core shaft](../item/components)

### Level 2 

- [Artron fluid link](../item/components)
- [Artron mercurial link](../item/components)
- [T](../item/components)[ranswarp resonator](../item/components)
- [Matrix energizer](../block/matrix-energizer)

### Level 3 

- Plasmic material
- Artron cable
- Photon accelerator
- Console generator

### Level 4 

- Generalized subsystem core
- Blueprint
- Orthogonal engine filter
- Waypoint catridge
- Waypoint bank

### Level 5 

- Gold key upgrade smithing template
- Oxygenator block
- Landing pad
- Redstone control block
