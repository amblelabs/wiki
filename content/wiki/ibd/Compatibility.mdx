# Are there any mods which add new functionality?

## **Simple Voice Chat**:
With the inclusion of Simple Voice Chat, players are granted the ability to use the ring comms function to communicate with their teammates in-game, as well as verbally saying a given corps' oath when recharging or synchronizing their ring at a lantern or core battery.
