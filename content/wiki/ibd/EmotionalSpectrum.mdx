## What is the emotional spectrum?

The emotional spectrum is an energy field from which various groups or individuals draw power. This includes augmenting one's own natural abilities, forming hard-light constructs, as well as flight and protecting oneself in the vacuum of space.


## How does the emotional spectrum work?

By having a ring equipped or held in hand, players are able to draw from that ring's attuned colour to perform feats one would not typically be able to. There are seven main parts to the emotional spectrum:

- [Red (Rage)](./Rings/Red.mdx)

- [Orange (Avarice/Greed)](./Rings/Orange.mdx)

- [Yellow (Fear)](./Rings/Yellow.mdx)

- [Green (Willpower)](./Rings/Green.mdx)

- [Blue (Hope)](./Rings/Blue.mdx)

- [Indigo (Compassion)](./Rings/Indigo.mdx)

- [Violet/Pink (Love)](./Rings/Pink.mdx)

Each colour has an associated emotion stat that ranges from 0 to 1000. Various actions may be taken to increase these stats, and they will all gradually decrease back to zero over time, at different rates. Rage, Avarice, and Fear drain the fastest, whilst Willpower, Hope, Compassion, and Love drain the slowest.

See each ring's page for further information on its associated stat.

## What rings can choose me?

Of all the colours on the spectrum, only three have rings that will seek out bearers. Red, Orange, and Green are all able to seek out a suitable bearer, assuming they have also met the requirements for their desired ring.

[Red:](./Rings/Red.mdx)
- Have at least 600 rage score
- Have no ring sworn at the time

[Orange:](./Rings/Orange.mdx)
- Have at least 250 greed score
- Have a hoard of riches
- Have no ring sworn at the time

OR

- Have 1000 greed score
- Have a hoard of riches
- Have no ring sworn at the time
- Be ready to fight a presently assigned Orange Lantern

[Green:](./Rings/Green.mdx)
- Have at least 600 willpower score
- Have no ring sworn at the time

Note: when seeking out a Red Power Ring, there is a 25% chance per day to be chosen for a ring, assuming you meet the above criteria and the corps maximum player cap is not met on the server. The Orange criteria being split between the two options depends on whether or not the Orange Lantern member cap is met.
