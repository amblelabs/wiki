## How do i use abilities?

The default keybinds are **R, X, Z** to use abilities.

Here are the steps to use an ability:

1. Hold either **R** for constructs or **Z** for utility abilities.
2. Hold the cursor over the ability you want to use.
3. Click **X** to hold out your arm
4. Hold 'Right Click' to use the ability.
