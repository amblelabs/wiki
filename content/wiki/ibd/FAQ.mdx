## How do I use abilities?

The default keybinds are:
- <Kbd>R</Kbd>: opens construct radial menu
- <Kbd>C</Kbd>: opens intrinsics radial menu
- <Kbd>G</Kbd>: dismiss most recent active construct. Press again to dismiss prior constructs in order of summoning.
- <Kbd>X</Kbd>: raises and lowers your ring-bearing arm

To use ring abilities:

- Hold either <Kbd>R</Kbd> for constructs or <Kbd>C</Kbd> for intrinsic abilities radial menu
- Hold the cursor over the ability you want to use and release the previous keybind
- Press <Kbd>X</Kbd> to raise your arm
- Hold **Right Mouse Button** to charge the ability.
Some abilities will upkeep themselves

Note that to access subfolders, you must move your cursor further in the direction of the given subfolder. Subfolders are indicated with three dots adjacent to their icon.


## How do I equip a ring?

In the inventory there is a **Ring Menu** to the left of the helmet slot. You may click a held ring onto this menu to equip it, shift right-click with a ring in hand, or open the ring menu and place it within the ring slot.

## Do I need to build a Central Power Battery for the **Orange Corps?**

No, due to Orange being the corps of Greed the lantern you are given is actually the Central Power Battery!


## Can I be chosen for another ring if I already have one?

No, If you are sworn to a ring you cannot be chosen for another one unless you relinquish your ring in the emotional spectrum menu.
