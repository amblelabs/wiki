## What are Parallax Shards used for?

Parallax Shards are one of the key items needed to **forge anything** in the [Yellow corps](../Rings/Yellow).

## Where do I find Parallax Shards?

Parallax Shards can be found in **Ancient City loot chests or from killing wardens**.
