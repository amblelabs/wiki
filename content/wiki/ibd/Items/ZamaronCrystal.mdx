## What are Zamaron Crystals?

Zamaron Crystals are an item needed to forge rings and lanterns for the [Star Sapphire (pink)](../Rings/Pink) Corps.

## How do I get Zamaron Crystals?

Zamaron Crystals are obtained the same way as a Zamaronian Crystal, from the meteor.

When a meteor is falling, a message will be broadcast to all players:

![Zamaron Message](/public/images/zamaronmessage.png)
