## What are milestones?

Milestones require one to undergo certain tasks in order to better attune to a given emotion. There are currently three milestones per emotion, completing each milestone increases maximum ring charge as well as the number of available constructs for a given emotion

- Milestones are randomly assigned based on the individual, and cannot be modified, changed, or skipped, without using commands or operator permissions on a server
- Most milestones can be assigned to any emotion, and can be viewed in the Emotional Spectrum menu(see: default kebind is **K**)
- The difficulty of a given milestone may not necessarily reflect which tier it is assigned to
