## What are milestones?

Milestones are a feature which makes you rank up in your corp. Ranking up has many benifits:

1. At Rank 4 you can break your battery unless you were the one who built it and couldve broken it from the start
2. New abilities are unlocked, these can be things like combat abilites or new constructs

## Why are Milestones so hard?

It depends on what quest you have. Many can be confusing

Thankfully, if you have OP you can reset or change the milestones for someone or yourself.
