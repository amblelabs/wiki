## This Page Talks about the Emotional Spectrum click **K** to see your levels.

The Blue (Hope) Ring is obtained very easily.

To get the Blue Ring you need to follow the shrines that start at spawn.

 These Shrines usually take you 2-3K blocks out

You **CANNOT** fly or teleport while doing the pilgrimage as it then stops it.

While doing the pilgrimage you must also have 250 Hope to get into the Temple, 

the shrines should give hope each time you pass one giving you 250 hope by the end

When you get to the end you must climb to the top of the Temple and touch the glowing blue insignia at the top to be granted a ring.


## You will also need to build a Central Power Battery to start using your ring.
