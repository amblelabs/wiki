## This page talks about the emotional spectrum(see: default kebind is K)

The Blue (Hope) Ring is among the "easier" rings to obtain. To get the Blue ring you need to follow the pilgrimage shrines leading away from spawn. These shrines will take you 2-3K blocks out, their trail ending at a temple surrounded by blue particles, adorned at the top by an alien symbol. You **cannot fly or teleport** while doing the pilgrimage or your participation will be revoked, and you will need to start from the beginning. Passing a shrine during the pilgrimage will give you some hope score, and by the end of the pilgrimage you will have at least 250 hope. In order to begin using your blue power ring, you will also need to build a Blue Power Battery.


## How do I get Hope?

- Follow the pilgrimage shrines. +8
- Plant crops or saplings. +1
- Use bone meal on any plant or block that consumes it. This includes growing flowers from grass, or accelerating crop growth. +1
- Cure zombie villagers. +100

## Available Constructs
Blue Lanterns, when standing alone, aren't suited to combat. Instead, they boost the power of Green and Indigo lanterns when nearby. As a result of this boost, the present Blue Lanterns also gain additional offensive and utility constructs such as blast and the drill constructs. Blue Lanterns are best suited to act as support members with their massive passive health regeneration bonus, as well as their healing beam construct. Yellow and Red Lanterns also receive a debuff when in the presence of a Blue Lantern, and a Blue Lantern is required to remove a red ring from its bearer.
