## This page talks about the emotional spectrum(see: default kebind is K)

The Green (Willpower) Ring may be obtained by meeting the following criteria:

- Have greater than 600 willpower score
- Have less than 250 fear or rage score
- Have no other ring sworn to you whilst waiting to be chosen by the Guardians of Oa

Upon meeting the above criteria, a countdown will begin, and you will have a random chance of getting the ring per in-game hour. This chance, while usually not negligible, can still end up being somewhat low, so patience is key when trying to obtain the green ring. Your chance to receive the green ring can be checked by using the command `/brightestday odds [your username] [ring desired]`. Like any other ring, you will need to construct a Green Power Battery before your green ring may be used.


## How do I get Willpower?

- Have less than three hearts(less than 6hp) and deal damage to mobs or players
- Defeat vanilla bosses; modded bosses may be included by addons in the future
