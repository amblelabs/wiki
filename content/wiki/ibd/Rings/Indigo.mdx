## This Page Talks about the Emotional Spectrum click **K** to see your levels.

The Indigo (Compassion) Ring is obtained by these simple steps:

1. Get your **Compassion** to atleast **250** on the spectrum screen.
2. Either be a blue lantern and be converted by another Indigo 
3. or use a Blue and Pink ring in a **Spectrum Forge**
## You will also need to build a Central Power Battery to start using your ring.
