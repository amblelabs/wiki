## This page talks about the emotional spectrum(see: default kebind is K)

The Indigo (Compassion) Ring is obtained by meeting the following criteria:

- Craft an Indigo ring by merging a Pink and Blue ring together at a Spectrum Forge

OR

- have 250 compassion score OR less than three hearts
- be converted by another Indigo using their Conversion ability

Like any other ring, you will need to construct an Indigo Power Battery before your indigo ring may be used.


## How do I get Compassion?

- Kill mobs that are targeting another player
- Give items to another play who is at low health

## Available Constructs
In addition to the below-listed construct abilities, Indigo Lanterns have the unique abilities to copy constructs from any ring bearer nearby, as well as convert other ring bearers with sufficient compassion score or low enough health. Red Lanterns may not be converted at any time, unless they relinquish their red ring and don another corps' ring. Targets for conversion must be wearing a non-red ring to be eligible.
