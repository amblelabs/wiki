## This Page Talks about the Emotional Spectrum click **K** to see your levels.

The Orange (Greed) Ring is obtained by these simple steps:

1. Have a Hoard of Riches (A collective value of 256)

Gold Ingot +1

Gold Block +9

Diamond +4

Diamond Block +36

Ancient Debris +8

Netherite Ingot +36

Netherite Block +324

2. Have 250 Greed

3. Wait to be granted the ring

OR

**Kill** someone that already has the orange ring with these requirements:

1. Have a **Hoard of riches** (A collective value of 256)
2. Have **Max** Greed
3. Be in possesion of **No** ring

## How do I get Greed?

Mine Diamonds or Ancient Debris
