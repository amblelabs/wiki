## This page talks about the emotional spectrum(see: default kebind is K)

The Orange (Greed) Ring can be obtained as such:

- Have a hoard of riches (a total value of at least 256)
- Have a greed score of at least 250
- Wait to be granted an orange ring

OR

Kill someone that already has the orange ring whilst meeting these requirements:

- Have a hoard of riches (a total value of at least 256)
- Have 1000 greed score
- Don't be sworn to a ring

## How do I get Greed?

| Action | Value |
| -------- | -------- |
| Mine Emerald | 10 |
| Mine Diamond | 30 |
| Mine Ancient Debris | 60 |

## Notes with regards to the Orange Corps:
Orange Lanterns do not have a central power battery, instead carrying their central battery with them as a lantern. As such, their ring compass ability is modified to instead point to the last location the Orange Power Battery was placed in the world. In addition to this, Orange Lanterns have a hoard value attributed to their inventory. The higher the hoard value, the slower the carrier. If the hoard value falls below a certain threshold, your ring will tell you it hungers for a larger hoard; in this case, your hoard value will be displayed on-screen and your ring's charge will drain faster.
