## This Page Talks about the Emotional Spectrum click **K** to see your levels.

The Red (Rage) Ring is obtained by these simple steps:

1. Kill animals to get Rage
2. Get your **Rage** to atleast **600** on the spectrum screen.
3. Wait to be chosen by the Guardians

## You will also need to build a Central Power Battery to start using your ring.

## You can also do the command `/brightestday odds [Player] [Ring]` to see when you may get a ring.
