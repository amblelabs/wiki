## This Page Talks about the Emotional Spectrum click **K** to see your levels.

The White (Life) Ring is obtained by these simple steps:

1. Balance all emotions within in the specified ranged mentioned in the Emotional Spectrum menu (a minimum of 500 for each emotion, NOTE: Your emotions must be balanced within a range of 250 from one another. I.e if your highest emotional value is 1000, your lowest emotional value can be no less than 750.)
2. Obtain all Rings, can be constructs as well
3. Drop all rings on top of each other to obtain the White Ring

## The White Ring's duration may vary between 22 and 30 minutes, based on emotional score spread at time of fusing rings
