## This page talks about the emotional spectrum, click **K** to see your levels.

The White Lantern Ring can be obtained once the following criteria are met:

- All emotional spectrum scores(see: menu bound by default to K) are above 500
- The difference between the highest and lowest emotional spectrum score does not exceed 250. E.g. if your highest score is 1000, the lowest cannot be below 750
- You have obtained at least one of every ring in the main spectrum; construct rings are valid rings for this purpose, regardless of duration

Upon meeting the above criteria, one can drop all seven rings onto the ground and the fusion process will start. Note that fused White rings will have a tooltip stating that they are fused, as well as a timer stating the remaining duration of the fusion.

The White Ring's duration may vary between 22 and 30 minutes, based on emotional score spread at time of fusing rings. If one should keep their Willpower(Green), Hope(Blue), Compassion(Indigo), and Love(Pink) scores above a certain threshold, the fused ring will remain active until that threshold is no longer met.
