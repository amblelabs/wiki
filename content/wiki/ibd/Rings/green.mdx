# This Page Talks about the Emotional Spectrum click K to see your levels.

The Green (Willpower) Ring is obtained by these simple steps:

1. Get your *Willpower* to atleast *600* on the spectrum screen.
2. Try not to have Fear or Rage *past 250*.
3. Simply *wait* for the guardians to choose you.

## You can also do the command `/brightestday odds [Player] [Ring]`
