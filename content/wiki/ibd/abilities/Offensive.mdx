## Combat abilities:

### Blast
Blast is a variable-charge ability that deals light impact damage and high area-of-effect (aoe) damage. Before firing, the user is able to select which level of Blast to use. The levels range from 1 to 5, with Blue Lantern boost providing up to 1 additional level to Green Lanterns present. Each increase in level also increases the damage dealt both in direct hit and aoe.

### Swarm Missiles

Swarm Missiles fires multiple homing projectiles that each do a flat damage amount per individual missile. These missiles will travel a fixed distance from the caster before disappearing. This construct is unique to Yellow and White Lanterns, with Indigo Lanterns being able to replicate it when within proximity of the aforementioned. Note that due to the nature of firing multiple projectiles at once, if more than one missile targets the same target, it is highly likely that only the damage of one missile will be applied. This is due to the nature of Minecraft: Java Edition combat invincibility frames, and is not inherently a bug within the mod itself.

| Swarm Missile Stat | Stat Value |
| -------- | -------- |
| Damage Per Missile | 10.1 |
| Range | 32 |
| Charge Cost | 150 |
| # of missiles | 10 |
| Homing FOV | 70 |
| Knockback | 0.25 |

### Ground Slam

Ground Slam, as the name implies, slams the user downward into the ground or nearest other surface. Per each block traveled, the ability increases damage slightly, adding up greatly over large distances. The shockwave applied on impact also increases in radius based on distance traveled to reach the ground.
