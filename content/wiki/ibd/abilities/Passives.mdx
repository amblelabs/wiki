## What is a synergy?

A synergy is what results when certain colours of lantern are present within a certain radius of one another. For example, if a Green Lantern and Blue Lantern are within a radius of one another, they both receive buffs to their ring efficiency.


### Synergy interactions:
| Synergy Interaction | Synergy Effect | Synergy Range |
| -------- | -------- | -------- |
| Full Potential | Greens have no upkeep costs, gain empowered constructs | 96 blocks |
| Lighter Burden | Indigo rings spend 75% less energy on actions | 96 blocks |
| Hope Prevails | Yellow, Red, Orange, energy costs doubled | 16 blocks |
| Fear Incarnate | Greens not under Hope Boost receive debuffed constructs | 16 blocks |
