# What does the Fear Power Battery Look like?

The Fear Battery is made out of gold blocks with a Yellow Battery Core in the middle.

Yellow Battery Core Recipe:

"Needs 250 Fear

Costs 2 lava

Only at night, away from light

Strike 5 beats; misses just retry the strike"

[Parallax Shards](../item/parallax-shards.mdx) are required for crafting.
