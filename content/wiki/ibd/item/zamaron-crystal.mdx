## What are Zamaron Crystals?

Zamaron Crystals are an item needed to forge rings and lanterns for the [Star Sapphire (pink)](../ring/pink) Corps.

## How do I get Zamaron Crystals?

Zamaron Crystals are obtained the same way as a Zamaronian Crystal, from the meteor. The meteor will fall every 12 days of in-game time.

When a meteor is falling, a message will be broadcast to all players:
