## This page talks about the emotional spectrum(see: default kebind is K)

The Red (Rage) Ring is obtained by meeting the following criteria:

- Have greater than 600 rage score
- Have less than 250 love score
- Have no other ring sworn to you whilst waiting to be chosen by the ring

Upon meeting the above criteria, a countdown will begin, and you will have a 25% chance of getting the ring per in-game day. Both ring selection percentages(red and green) can be viewed by using the command `/brightestday odds [your username] [ring desired]`. Like any other ring, you will need to construct a [Red Power Battery](../battery/RedBattery.mdx) before your red ring may be used.

## How do I get Rage?

| Action | Value |
| -------- | -------- |

| Kill animal | 25 |

## Notes with regards to the Red Corps:

Red Lanterns have less overall utility than other corps members, being mostly geared toward expressing rage through combat. As such, Red Lanterns have access to less constructs, and those they do are primarily combative abilities. Reds also gain a damage boost proportional to the amount of rage score the user has. Red Lanterns gain blood charge meter by dealing damage, or killing mobs/players. Blood charge meter is consumed to make many constructs, and most constructs are unavailable when the blood charge meter is empty.


## Available Constructs
In addition to these below-listed construct abilities, Red Lanterns also have access to Acid Vomic, a projectile ability that spews caustic fluid. They also gain Berserk, which drains your rage to zero. During this drain period it grants improved stats. In addition to these abilities, when a Red Lantern uses their launch takeoff boost, it does damage to anyone the user collides with.
