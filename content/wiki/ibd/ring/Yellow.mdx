## This page talks about the emotional spectrum(see: default kebind is K)

The Yellow (Fear) Ring can be obtained as such:

- Craft a Spectrum Forge. You need 250 Fear to start forging, and forging can only be performed at night.
- Get the correct materials for the ring and lantern. See materials by shift right-clicking the block, it will list the required materials on-screen.
- Complete the minigame to forge your ring and lantern. You can complete the minigame by left or right clicking in time with the visual

Like any other ring, you will need to construct a [Yellow Power Battery](../battery/YellowBattery.mdx) before your yellow ring may be used. Note that in order to provide lava for the recipes, you must right-click the Spectrum Forge with a bucket of lava; a Spectrum Forge can hold up to 4 lava at any given time, though it may not be reclaimed. [Parallax Shards](../item/ParallaxShards.mdx) are required for crafting.

![Spectrum Forge Recipe](/images/spectrum_forge_recipe.png)

Recipe for crafting a Yellow Ring:

"Needs 250 Fear

Costs 1 lava

Only at night, away from light

Strike 5 beats; misses just retry the strike"


![Yellow Ring Recipe](/images/yellow_ring_recipe.png)

Recipe for crafting a Yellow Lantern:

"Needs 250 Fear

Costs 2 lava

Only at night, away from light

Strike 3 beats; misses just retry the strike"


![Yellow Lantern Recipe](/images/yellow_lantern_recipe.png)


## How do I get Fear?

| Action | Value |
| -------- | -------- |
| Damage animal | 5 |
| Attack target that wasn't facing or targeting you | 12 |
| Kill mob that is fleeing, panicking, wounded, weakened, or slowed | 15 |
| Kill whilst sneaking or invisible | 20 |
| Kill wounded or fleeing player | 60 |
