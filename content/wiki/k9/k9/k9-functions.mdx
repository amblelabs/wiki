Healing K9

K9 is fed and can be healed with redstone, or `right clicked` with a wrench to get to full health. Remember that health and battery are separate.
## Abilites

All the features of a regular dog, except for the incessant begging.
- K9 goes to items and picks them up.
- K9 can be sat on hoppers and get items extracted from it's inventory.
- K9 has an inventory, like a shulker box on wheels!
- K9 has a dyeable collar, and an easter egg (naming him bugger) if you decide to fool around a little bit!
