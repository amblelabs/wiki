## What is a DHD?
- A DHD is a device that is used to dial addresses for Stargates. If you press any symbol on the block the Stargate it is linked too will activate that same symbol. When you reach the max amount of symbols for that address, you then press the big red button in the middle and the gate will activate.
- You can also use an [Address Cartouche](../item/address-cartouche/) on a DHD to automatically dial that address (you will however still need to press the activation button)!
