## What does the Address Cartouche do?
- It can be used to get a Stargates address (by `right-clicking` on the Stargates base block) and can be used on both the [DHD](../block/dhd/) And [Stargate](../block/stargate/). It will then automatically dial the address but it wont activate the Stargate when it has complete its dialing sequence!
