We use extended SGA (standard galactic alphabet), which currently counts 36 unique symbols. Gate's addresses are split into 2 categories: `local` and `global`.
## LOCAL:
- Local addresses are 7 chevron addresses and 8 chevron addresses. The first 6 symbols of the address are the `id` . The 7th symbol of the local address is the point of origin, 7 chevron addresses only target gates in the same dimension, where as 8 chevron addresses target gates in other dimensions. Local addresses only have 7 symbols, however, when dialing another dimension, the 7th symbol is the target dimension and 8th is the dimension you are in.
## GLOBAL:
- Global addresses are 9 chevron addresses , the first 8 symbols of the global address is the `id` and the 9th one is target dimension..
